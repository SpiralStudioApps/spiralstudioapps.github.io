How to block apps on a schedule on Android

Spiral Studio · October 2026

Willpower is weakest at the same times every day: the first hour after waking, the work block after lunch, the last hour before sleep. A schedule takes the decision away. The apps close on their own at 9 pm, and you never have to argue with yourself about it. Here's what Android offers, and what to look for if you need something firmer.

1. Focus mode with a schedule (built in)

Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → Focus mode. Choose the distracting apps, then Set a schedule: the days and the start and end times. While it's on, those apps are greyed out and their notifications are held back.

Good: free, built in, and it handles weekdays and weekends separately. Not so good: it's designed to be easy to leave. "Take a break" or "Turn off now" sits right on the notification, so it works only on days you didn't really need it.

2. Bedtime mode

Also under Digital Wellbeing. On a schedule it turns the screen grey, switches on Do Not Disturb and dims the wallpaper. It makes the phone less appealing at night, but it doesn't block anything: every app still opens.

3. Samsung Modes and Routines

On Samsung phones, Settings → Modes and Routines lets you create a mode (Sleep, Work, Study) that switches on automatically at set times. Modes are good at silencing notifications and changing settings. They don't keep a determined thumb out of Instagram.

4. What a schedule needs to actually hold

If the built-in options are too easy to skip, a dedicated app can lock things down on a timetable. Before you trust one, check four things:

  • It starts on time. Android delays ordinary background alarms to save battery. A schedule needs exact alarms, or 9:00 becomes 9:14.
  • It survives a restart. Restarting the phone is the oldest trick there is. Both the running lock and the schedule itself should come back after a reboot.
  • You can't simply end it early. Otherwise you've rebuilt Focus mode.
  • Calls still get through. A lock that blocks your family in an emergency won't stay installed for long.
