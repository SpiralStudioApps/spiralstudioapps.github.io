How to stop doomscrolling at night on Android

Spiral Studio · October 2026

You get into bed meaning to check one thing. Forty minutes later you're deep in videos you won't remember, and tomorrow starts tired. Late at night is the worst time to rely on willpower: you're tired, the feed never ends, and nothing tells you to stop. The fix is to make the decision earlier in the day, while you're still awake enough to make it.

1. Bedtime mode (built in)

Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → Bedtime mode. Set your bedtime and it turns the screen grey, switches on Do Not Disturb and dims the wallpaper. It's a good signal that the day is over. It doesn't stop anything: every app still opens, just in grey, and you can switch it off from the quick settings.

2. Grayscale

Colour is a large part of why feeds feel rewarding. A grey Instagram is noticeably less interesting. You can turn on grayscale through Bedtime mode, or keep it as an Accessibility shortcut (Settings → Accessibility → Colour correction → Grayscale). It helps, but it's one tap to undo, and a tired thumb will find that tap.

3. App timers

A Digital Wellbeing timer can cap Instagram or TikTok at an hour a day. Two problems at night: once the app is paused you can delete the timer in a few taps, and the timers reset at midnight, so the scrolling after 12 counts as a brand new day.

4. Focus mode on a schedule

Focus mode can pause your chosen apps every night from, say, 10:30 pm. It's the closest built-in option to a real bedtime block, but "Take a break" sits right on the notification. At 11:40 pm, you'll take the break.

5. The analog fix

Charging the phone in another room and using a cheap alarm clock still works better than almost any setting. If you can do it, do it. If you can't (you need the phone nearby for calls or for your alarm), the next best thing is a lock that starts on its own.

What a bedtime lock needs

  • It starts by itself. At 10:30 pm you shouldn't have to decide anything. The decision was made in the afternoon.
  • It lasts until morning and can't be ended early. If it can be cancelled, it will be.
  • It survives a restart. Restarting the phone is the first thing a tired brain tries.
  • Calls and your alarm still work. Otherwise you won't keep it, and you shouldn't.
