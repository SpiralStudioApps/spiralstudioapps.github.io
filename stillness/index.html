Four ways to disconnect
01 — FULL LOCK

The whole phone goes quiet.

Anything from five minutes to a full day. Calls and emergency calls still come through; everything else waits until the time is up. Even a restart won't end it.

02 — SELECTIVE LOCK

Quiet the loud apps. Keep the rest.

Block what pulls you in: social media, games, news, the browser. Messages, maps and music keep working.

03 — DAILY LIMITS

Every app gets a bedtime.

Give YouTube fifteen minutes a day. When it's spent, the app rests until 4 am. Android counts the time, so a restart can't reset it, and strict mode won't let you loosen a resting limit.

04 — SCHEDULES

Rhythms that keep themselves.

Morning prayer, family dinner, a calm bedtime. Set the rhythm once and Stillness begins on its own.

Questions

Good to know.

Can people still call me during a lock?

Yes. Incoming calls and emergency calls get through in every mode, and alarms still ring. You can also save emergency contacts to call from the lock screen.

What happens if I restart my phone?

The session comes back after the restart and carries on until its end time. Daily Limits are counted by Android itself, so a restart doesn't reset them either.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone on Android 8.0 or newer. Some brands (Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus and others) aggressively close background apps. If a lock ever slips, allowing Stillness to run in the background in your battery settings usually fixes it. Tell us your phone model and we'll look into it.

Why does it ask for Accessibility and Device Admin?

Accessibility lets Stillness see which app is in the foreground so it can keep a blocked app closed. It reads only the app's name, never your screen or what you type. Device Admin is what lets Full Lock actually lock the phone. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both lock modes, a schedule, a season, a daily limit and the Ocean theme are free forever, with no ads. Premium removes the limits and unlocks every theme.
