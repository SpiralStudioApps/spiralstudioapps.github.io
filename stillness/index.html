Full Lock

The whole phone goes quiet. Calls and emergency calls still come through; everything else waits until the timer ends.

Questions

Good to know

Can people still call me during a lock?

Yes. Incoming calls and emergency calls get through in every mode, and alarms still ring. You can also save emergency contacts you can call from the lock screen.

What happens if I restart my phone?

The session comes back after the restart and carries on until its end time. Daily Limits are counted by Android itself, so a restart doesn't reset them either.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone on Android 8.0 or newer. Some brands (Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus and others) aggressively close background apps. If a lock ever slips, allowing Stillness to run in the background in your battery settings usually fixes it. Tell us your phone model and we'll look into it.

How is this different from Digital Wellbeing or Forest?

Digital Wellbeing's timers are easy to switch off when you're tempted, and Forest is a gamified timer rather than a real lock. Stillness actually locks the phone or the apps you choose, holds through restarts, and is designed to feel calm rather than like a punishment.

Why does it ask for Accessibility and Device Admin permissions?

Accessibility lets Stillness see which app is in the foreground so it can keep a blocked app closed. Device Admin is what lets Full Lock actually lock the phone. Neither is used for anything else, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both lock modes, a schedule, a season, a daily limit and the Ocean theme are free forever, with no ads. Premium removes the limits and unlocks every theme.
