Eclipse, now and with a real corona

Both phones play the app's 30-second arrival: the sun, the thinning crescent, Baily's beads, the diamond ring, then totality. On the right: a real photographed corona; the moon's mountainous edge breaking the last light into Baily's beads; a softer diamond ring over the first ring of corona; a pink flash at the edge and prominences that stay; a limb-darkened sun whose glare shrinks with the crescent; earthshine and Venus. The beads and ring now take about four seconds instead of one.

The home screen orb

Press and hold an orb, like starting a lock. Night falls over both; on the right the corona is the real one, and the moment the lock begins is the diamond ring.
